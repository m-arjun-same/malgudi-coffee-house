What makes it Malgudi

done properly

01

Filter coffee, the real way

Strong, authentic South Indian filter coffee, served in traditional brass tumblers — hot enough to spark memories and conversations.

02

Sabudana Wada — the star

Reviewers name it the star item of the shop: crisp sabudana wada, three pieces for ₹40, fried fresh through the day.

03

Fresh-fried snacks

Samosas, punugulu, masala wada and pakodas lined up in the glass display case — classic snacks, fresh and flavourful.

A corner of Malgudi Days

A vintage nook inside KPHB Metro Station — aged wooden tables, cane-back chairs and décor that feels like stepping into R.K. Narayan's Malgudi.

Good to know

Questions,
answered

01Where exactly is the shop?

Inside KPHB Metro Station, on the Concourse Level — K P H B Phase 1, Kukatpally, Hyderabad 500072. Step off the metro and the counter is right there in the station.

02When is it open?

Every day, 7:30 AM to 10:30 PM — morning commuters and evening travellers alike.

03What is it famous for?

South Indian filter coffee in brass tumblers, and the star snack — sabudana wada. Dum chai, thatte idli, punugulu and nendram chips round out the regulars' list.

04How much will I spend?

Very little — the menu runs from ₹25 to ₹80, and listings put a visit at roughly ₹1–200 per person. Filter coffee is ₹35.

05Can I sit in, or only take away?

Both. It's a small, cozy dine-in nook with aged tables and chairs — and everything travels well if your train is calling.
